06FAQ

Before we build anything.

The goal is to make the first message useful. You do not need to prepare a technical specification.

Do I need a call before starting?

No. You can send the full project brief through the contact page. We’ll review it and reply by message/email with the recommended scope, key integrations, and next step.

Can you work with tools I already use?

Usually, yes. The exact answer depends on the tool’s API, webhooks, permissions, and how much control it gives the automation. We scope this before the build.

Do you only build chatbots?

No. Chatbots, lead-generation systems, and workflow automation are the three core offers. A project can combine them when the business process actually requires it.

Is the monthly support fee required?

No. Support is optional. The base build includes testing and handoff. Ongoing monitoring, tuning, and updates can be added when needed.

What if I do not know what should be automated?

That is exactly what the project brief is for. Describe the current process, where the manual work happens, and what you want to improve. We’ll identify the sensible automation boundary.

Have a process that feels too manual?

Send the context. We’ll tell you what we would automate, what we would leave alone, and what the project would roughly require.
